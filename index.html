The Night the Estate Burned

You awaken to the smell of smoke and the sound of screams. Your clan estate is under violent attack. When an enemy vanguard breaks through into your bedchamber, you must choose: flee into the night or stand and fight.

The Radiant Mother is not a goddess. She is a celestial parasite — and she has just cursed you with The Inverse. You are the first soul she could not eat. You will be the last.

What You Are About to Play

A dark fantasy tactical RPG prologue — built around a single question: what happens when a goddess rejects you, and something older catches you on the way down?

You Are Not The Chosen One

You are the soul too frail to harvest. The goddess looked at you and found you unworthy. That rejection is your only weapon.

The Inverse

Your stats are negative. Your soul is a void. Every champion who falls to you becomes a meal she cannot claim.

Five Chapters, One Road

From the ashes of your clan to the shrine of the Radiant Mother at Owari. Every chapter brings you closer. Every choice feeds something.

A Voice In The Dark

The goddess the world forgot is watching through your eyes. She was here before the Radiant Mother. She will be here after.
